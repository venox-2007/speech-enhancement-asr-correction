# Milestone 5 — Context-Aware ASR Correction Evaluation

## Executive Summary

This milestone evaluated the context-aware post-ASR correction pipeline across the complete **VoiceBank+DEMAND held-out test partition** ($N=824$ utterances, $6,211$ reference words). We evaluated downstream Whisper `base.en` hypotheses in two pipeline configurations:
1. **Noisy Speech $\to$ Whisper $\to$ Correction Pipeline**
2. **Enhanced Speech (U-Net) $\to$ Whisper $\to$ Correction Pipeline**

All evaluations used the cached Whisper transcriptions from `results/asr_enhancement_eval/asr_cache.json` to eliminate non-deterministic ASR variations and preserve exact comparability. Correction was executed with the default configuration established in earlier milestones (`threshold=0.70`, `phonetic_weight=1.0`, `max_candidates=20`, `gpt2` language model) without test-set parameter tuning.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Key Empirical Findings**:
> - **The correction mechanism works as designed**: It successfully identified and repaired **19 genuine ASR errors** in noisy speech and **24 genuine ASR errors** in enhanced speech (e.g., *gullible* $\to$ *culpable*, *easier* $\to$ *easy*, *hes* $\to$ *his*, *mcdonald* $\to$ *macdonald*).
> - **Overcorrections outnumbered successful fixes**: Because GPT-2 prioritizes high-frequency n-grams without acoustic posterior weighting, it modified **69 already-correct words** in noisy speech and **67 already-correct words** in enhanced speech (e.g., changing *explained* $\to$ *explored*, *difference* $\to$ *distance*, *bottle* $\to$ *battle*, *accident* $\to$ *incident*).
> - **Overall WER degraded slightly**:
>   - **Noisy Speech**: Raw Corpus WER **7.23%** $\to$ Corrected Corpus WER **8.03%** ($\Delta = \mathbf{+0.81\%}$, $+11.1\%$ relative error increase). Correction Precision = **16.96%**.
>   - **Enhanced Speech**: Raw Corpus WER **8.23%** $\to$ Corrected Corpus WER **8.89%** ($\Delta = \mathbf{+0.66\%}$, $+8.0\%$ relative error increase). Correction Precision = **20.17%**.
> - **Conclusion**: Without acoustic model confidence scores or tight vocabulary constraints, unconstrained LM rescoring at `threshold=0.70` introduces more spurious substitutions than it repairs.

## Experimental Setup & Methodology

### 1. Correction Pipeline Architecture

The correction pipeline operates on word-tokenized hypotheses generated by Whisper `base.en`:
1. **Phonetic Candidate Generation**:
   - For each word in the hypothesis, pronunciation variants are retrieved from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (`cmudict`).
   - Phonetically similar candidates within an edit distance of $\le 2$ phone operations are retrieved.
   - Up to $K=20$ candidate words are evaluated per target position.
2. **Context-Aware Rescoring**:
   - For each candidate $w_i'$, a composite score combines the phonetic similarity and the contextual language model likelihood:
     $$\text{Score}(w_i', \mathbf{w}_{\setminus i}) = \text{LM\_LogProb}(w_i' \mid \mathbf{w}_{\setminus i}) - \lambda \cdot \text{Dist}_{\text{phonetic}}(w_i, w_i')$$
   - The candidate with the highest composite score is selected.
   - If the improvement over the original word exceeds `threshold=0.70`, the word is replaced; otherwise, the original hypothesis word is preserved.
3. **Scoring Model**: HuggingFace `gpt2` (124M parameters, causal language model) evaluated in batched mode on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 6GB Laptop GPU (CUDA).

### 2. Evaluation Protocol & Ground Truth References

- **Test Set**: Official VoiceBank+DEMAND test set ($N=824$ utterances, $6,211$ words).
- **Speakers**: `p232` (male, 393 utterances, 2,991 words) and `p257` (female, 431 utterances, 3,220 words). Unseen during all training and validation stages.
- **Reference Transcripts**: Official `.txt` transcripts distributed with Edinburgh DataShare release.
- **Text Normalization**: Standard `jiwer` normalization (lowercase, punctuation stripped, whitespace normalized) applied identically to references, raw hypotheses, and corrected hypotheses.

### 3. Word Decision Taxonomy

Following rigorous ASR post-processing evaluation standards, word changes are classified by aligning raw hypotheses, corrected hypotheses, and ground-truth references:
- **Successful Correction ($S_{\text{corr}}$)**: The raw ASR word was an error (substitution), and the correction pipeline replaced it with the exact reference word.
- **Overcorrection ($O_{\text{corr}}$)**: The raw ASR word was correct (hit), but the correction pipeline replaced it with an incorrect word.
- **Failed Correction ($F_{\text{corr}}$)**: The raw ASR word was an error, and the correction pipeline modified it, but the result was still incorrect.
- **Unchanged Correct ($C_{\text{unchanged}}$)**: The raw word was correct and was left unchanged.
- **Unchanged Error ($E_{\text{unchanged}}$)**: The raw word was an error and was left unchanged.
- **Correction Precision**:
  $$\text{Precision} = \frac{S_{\text{corr}}}{S_{\text{corr}} + O_{\text{corr}} + F_{\text{corr}}} = \frac{\text{Successful Corrections}}{\text{Total Modifications}}$$

## Research Analysis & Lessons Learned

### 1. The Missing Link: Acoustic Posterior Confidence
In classic two-pass speech recognition architectures (e.g. WFST lattice rescoring or N-best list rescoring), post-processing incorporates the acoustic model's observation likelihood:
$$\hat{W} = \arg\max_W \left( \log P_{\text{acoustic}}(Y \mid W) + \alpha \log P_{\text{LM}}(W) \right)$$
In our independent post-ASR correction module, the acoustic likelihood $\log P_{\text{acoustic}}(Y \mid W)$ is absent. Whisper's internal token log-probabilities are discarded once the string is decoded. Consequently:
- Words that Whisper transcribed with **99.9% acoustic confidence** are treated with the same uncertainty as ambiguous, noisy phonemes.
- When an acoustic cue was loud, clear, and verbatim correct (e.g., *bottle*), an unconstrained LM can easily overturn it if the alternative has a slightly higher unigram/n-gram prior.

### 2. The Asymmetry of High-Accuracy Foundation Models
- When raw ASR WER is already low ($7.23\%$ for noisy speech, meaning $92.77\%$ of words are already correct), a blind candidate search visits **5,812 correct words** and only **399 erroneous words**.
- At a fixed false positive rate of even $1.2\%$ on correct words ($69 / 5,812$), the overcorrections will completely overwhelm the true error corrections ($19 / 399 \approx 4.8\%$ recall).
- Post-ASR rescoring without acoustic gating is only mathematically favorable when either:
  1. The baseline ASR error rate is very high ($> 30\%$), or
  2. The candidate generator is restricted exclusively to acoustic regions where Whisper's entropy/uncertainty is high.

### Core Conclusion
Both the signal-level magnitude U-Net and the post-ASR language model corrector demonstrate their targeted local capabilities (spectrogram noise removal and homophone/agreement repair, respectively). However, when evaluated on end-to-end downstream transcription accuracy against a self-supervised foundation model like Whisper:
1. Signal-level magnitude masking introduces out-of-distribution acoustic artifacts that increase Whisper's substitutions ($+1.00\%$ WER).
2. Pure post-ASR LM rescoring without acoustic confidence gating generates overcorrections that outweigh valid phonetic repairs ($+0.66\%$ to $+0.81\%$ WER).
These findings provide valuable, empirically verified research insights for deep learning speech systems.
